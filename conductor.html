About Our Conductors

Our conductors are manufactured with precision to ensure maximum efficiency, durability, and performance. They are suitable for power transmission and distribution applications, ensuring reliability even under extreme conditions. With high conductivity and mechanical strength, our conductors are designed to meet national and international standards.

ACSR (Aluminium Conductors Steel Reinforced) is a composite, concentrically stranded conductor in which the lightweight and good conductivity of aluminum are combined with the high tensile strength of steel.

ACSR has long been widely used as overhead high-tension transmission lines and has an established reputation for economy and dependability.
